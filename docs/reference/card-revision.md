# Card revision

A sequential, batch-by-batch revision of every implemented card text in the Standard-legal pool (Scarlet & Violet + Mega Evolution). Each batch's printed texts were audited against the engine's behaviour on a real board (smoke harness plus targeted probes), and the verified findings were then fixed, rejected or deferred on branch `card-revision`. A fix is accepted only when a test drives the real mechanism, asserts the printed text, fails before the change and passes after it. Recorded readings (R-xxx, UNRULED notes) are left as they are: the user deferred all rulings. Each section below lists the batch's findings, what happened to each one, and the test that covers it.

## B01 — Supporters

Engine 0.530.0 → 0.531.0. `MATCH_RECORD_VERSION` stays 49 (apps/api `match.ts`, "STAYS 49 AT CARD REVISION B01"). Suite: `packages/engine/src/revisionB01.test.ts`.

| Unit | Card | Severity | Outcome | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U0648 | Judge | bug | fixed | `B01 §1 — Judge: every Standard-legal printing runs the one program` |
| U0655 | Rosa's Encouragement | bug | fixed (shared op) | `B01 §2 — Rosa's Encouragement: the player picks WHICH Basic Energy cards` |
| U0347 | Grimsley's Move | minor | fixed (shared op) | `B01 §3 — Grimsley's Move: a full Bench is offered nothing` |

**U0648 Judge (`sv04.5-228`, `sv10-167`, `sv10-222`).** Three of the five printings had no registry row and were refused with `TRAINER_NOT_SIMULATED`. D290 had left them out on purpose while they were `legal_standard = 0`. Since 714256b3 they are `legal_standard_effective = 1` (same `print_key` as `me03-076`), so a Standard deck can include them. The fix adds the three ids as rows on the shipped `JUDGE` program. In `reprintAliasLegal.test.ts` they move out of the D290 refusal census into `LANDED_SINCE_EFFECTIVE_LEGALITY`, so the census counts become 43 identities / 78 printings, plus 62 and 87. The registry-size pins move from 1438 to 1441, `nonAttackRegistryIds` from 1273 to 1276, the D273 partition from 844 to 847 (the ids are in `CENSUS_NOT_LEGAL`, because their raw `legal_standard` is 0), and the `handRefresh` producers from 42 to 45. The other 43 census rows (Professor's Research, Iono, Rare Candy, Switch, Ultra Ball, Nemona, …) have the same effective-legality problem. They are owed to the batches that own those units.

**U0655 Rosa's Encouragement (and every untyped `attachEnergyFrom`).** *"Attach up to 2 Basic Energy cards from your discard pile"* attached the first matching cards in pile order. This rested on D32's assumption that "same-type Basic Energy is fungible", which does not hold when the printed noun has no type. The fix is in the shared op. `energyPickOffer` (interpreter.ts) checks whether the cards are a real decision: the pile must hold more attachable cards than the take, and more than one `cardIdentity` (a Basic Energy by its type, any other card by its print). If they are, the target answer (or the forced target) moves nothing. Instead the op is scheduled again with the new optional key `pickOnto { ref, take }`, and that step parks a `chooseCards` (`dest: "attach"`, `min = max =` the take) over every attachable card. The picked uids are what `attachEnergyFrom` attaches. A pile of one identity, or a pile no bigger than the take, still attaches inline with no second prompt. The fix reaches every untyped row: N's PP Up, Seething Spirit, Charging Up, Energy Assist, Empower, Pick and Stick, Lucky Attachment, But First, Food, Fist of Focus, Swelling Wish, Wrapped in Wind, Powerglass, Energizing Sketch, Overvolt Discharge and Mischievous Painting (the opponent's pile, answered by the attacker). Typed and named rows are unchanged. Rungs that pinned the old first-in-pile reading were re-driven to answer the card question: `attachEnergyFrom`, `coinGateMeD595` (its "KNOWN LIMITATION: the ENERGY is not chosen" rung is retired), `overvoltDischarge`, `opponentDiscardAttach` (ii)/(iii)/(ix)/(xi) and the `attachSeat(` count, `trainerParamsMeD588` §6, and `trainersD561`'s Crispin rollback proxy. Rollback reading: a 0.530 build resuming the card question attaches nothing for that one attach (a silent whiff), as recorded in match.ts. Three mutant rows were added: `B01-attach-card-never-asked`, `B01-attach-card-answer-ignored` and `B01-look-bench-offer-unclamped`. Each was applied by hand and killed by `revisionB01.test.ts`.

**U0347 Grimsley's Move (and every `lookAtTopN` with `dest: "bench"`).** With a full Bench, the top-7 look offered a {D} Basic that could not be benched, accepted the pick and benched nothing. The offer is now clamped to the free Bench space at the park (`offerMax`, `searchDeck`'s rule, on `lookOwner`'s Bench). A full Bench therefore takes the whiff path: the look is announced and all 7 cards go under the deck. The card stays playable, because the look and the reorder are effects. The apply's own clamp stays as the belt for a stale answer. Accompanying Flute's R-AF3 outcome (revealed, none taken, shuffled) is unchanged; only its parenthetical "the park is still offered" is retired. That was the carried debt D567 named: "a park-time Bench clamp … is NOT built". The `trainerBundleD567` PARTIAL / R-AF3 rungs and `derivedLookAtTop`'s Summoning Gate clamp rung now assert the capped offer. Buddy-Buddy Poffin into a full Bench (`searchDeck`, already clamped to `max` 0) is a separate, engine-wide question and was not changed here.

Checks: `bunx tsc -b` clean. `bunx biome lint` clean on the touched files. The full engine suite has no failures (the baseline also had none). The apps/api and web `src` suites are green. `bun scripts/mutation/precheck.ts` reports 4 broken rows that already exist at HEAD: `D455-optional-apply-arm-deleted`, plus three cache rows that still anchor `CACHE_VERSION = "v9"` after 714256b3 moved the cache to v11. None of them involve this batch. The full smoke run reports 0 unsimulated Supporter runs (6 before the fix) and no threw or stuck runs.

## B02 — Supporters

Engine 0.531.0 → 0.532.0. `MATCH_RECORD_VERSION` stays 49 (apps/api `match.ts`, "STAYS 49 AT CARD REVISION B02"). Suite: `packages/engine/src/revisionB02.test.ts`. Both findings were about wording only. The board outcome was already right in each case.

| Unit | Card | Severity | Outcome | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1164 | Caretaker | minor | fixed | `B02 §1 — Caretaker: the log names the played Caretaker going into the deck` |
| U0742 | Roxie's Performance | minor | fixed | `B02 §2 — Roxie's Performance: the refusal names a Trainer effect, not an attack` |

**U1164 Caretaker (`sv06-144`, `sv06-203`).** With Community Center in play and at least one card drawn, Caretaker went into the deck correctly. The log, however, said "shuffled 1 card from their discard pile into their deck", which contradicts the printed "instead of discarding it". `playTrainer` puts the Supporter in the discard pile before the program runs, so `shufflePlayedCardIntoDeck` (interpreter.ts) moves it from discard to deck and emitted the generic `DISCARD_RETRIEVED { dest: "deck" }`. The fix adds the optional key `played: true` to that event, written only by this op. log.ts renders it as "shuffled Caretaker into their deck instead of discarding it", and the `SHUFFLE` row still follows. Every other `DISCARD_RETRIEVED` producer is unchanged (Super Rod and the rest keep their row). `trainerBundleD564.test.ts` §6's exact-event rung now includes the key. The internal stop in the discard pile, within one action and with no event, is unchanged.

**U0742 Roxie's Performance (`me04-081`, `me04-112`, `me04-121`).** The lock already worked as printed: on the opponent's next turn their Poisoned Pokémon can't retreat, an unpoisoned one can, and the lock ends with that turn. The refusal, though, said "an attack effect prevents this Pokémon from retreating (§11)". turn.ts's §11 gate now asks `sideRetreatLocked` (continuous.ts, now exported). When the side lock holds the body and no attack lock does (`retreatBlocked` / `retreatLockedTurn`), the message is "a Trainer effect prevents Poisoned Pokémon from retreating this turn (§11)". The error code stays `RETREAT_PREVENTED`. An Antique Fossil keeps its own message, and a body that is also attack-locked keeps the attack message.

Two mutant rows were added, `B02-caretaker-row-as-retrieval` and `B02-side-lock-message-blames-attack`. Each was applied by hand and killed by `revisionB02.test.ts`. Smoke run on U1164 and U0742: 10 Supporter runs, all ok. The Caretaker log now reads "played Caretaker / drew 2 cards / shuffled Caretaker into their deck instead of discarding it / shuffled their deck".

## B04 — Items

Engine 0.532.0 → 0.533.0. `MATCH_RECORD_VERSION` stays 49 (apps/api `match.ts`, "STAYS 49 AT CARD REVISION B04"). Suite: `packages/engine/src/revisionB04.test.ts`.

| Unit | Card | Severity | Outcome | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U0252, U0257, U0258 | Rare Candy / Switch / Ultra Ball reprints | bug | fixed | `B04 §1/§2 — every Standard-legal printing of Rare Candy, Switch, Ultra Ball and Poké Pad runs its twin's program` |
| U0257, U0258, U0548 | `30th` Switch / Ultra Ball / Poké Pad | bug | fixed in part (play fixed; face text deferred) | same block (`Switch 30th-127`, `Ultra Ball 30th-128`, `Poké Pad 30th-126`) |
| U0252 | Rare Candy with no Stage 1 in either deck | bug | deferred | `B04 §3 — Rare Candy with no copy of the Stage 1 in either deck (DEFERRED: the pool builders)` |
| U0646 | Hole-Digging Shovel | minor | fixed | `B04 §4 — Hole-Digging Shovel: an empty deck refuses the Item` |

**U0252 / U0257 / U0258 — Rare Candy `sv04.5-089`, Switch `sv03.5-206` / `mfb-34`, Ultra Ball `sv04.5-091`.** This is B01's Judge defect again. These four printings had no registry row, so they were refused with `TRAINER_NOT_SIMULATED` (the `rareCandy` action said "Rare Candy is not Rare Candy"). Since 714256b3 they are `legal_standard_effective = 1`: each has `legal_standard = 0` but shares its print_key with an authored twin (`6716a9667c036c52`, `dcb764a74f0f46dc`, `9be1d8a4deedc7f9`). The text is byte-identical on the local D1. The fix adds rows on the shipped `RARE_CANDY`, `SWITCH` and `ULTRA_BALL` objects, after Judge's rows. In `reprintAliasLegal.test.ts` the three census rows move to `LANDED_SINCE_EFFECTIVE_LEGALITY`. The counts become 40 identities / 74 printings (58 outside D190's sample, 83 in the union), and the old "the fourth Rare Candy stays unbuilt" rung now asserts that all four share one program. The mutant rows `D290-registry-folds-the-fourth-rare-candy` and `D290-registry-folds-a-printing-D190-never-sampled` are retired, because their premise (0 Standard-legal) is gone. `B04-reprint-row-dropped` is the inverse experiment.

**U0257 / U0258 / U0548 — `30th-126` Poké Pad, `30th-127` Switch, `30th-128` Ultra Ball.** These are `legal_standard = 1`, but tcgdex gives them no text: `effect = NULL`, so their print_key matches no twin and no census reached them. They now have registry rows on `POKE_PAD`, `SWITCH` and `ULTRA_BALL`, so they play as printed even with the text-less face (the test faces carry `effect: null`, as the catalog does). In `censusAtHead.test.ts` they are recorded as `CENSUS_ME_TRAINER_B04`. They are the "3 left" outside every text column that D646 names, and no column moves. In `reprintAliasMeD582.test.ts` they are `KEYED_AT_B04`. **Deferred: the face text.** The card still shows no text, because the catalog row has none. Fixing that means an ingest-side backfill (copy the effect, and with it the print_key, from the one same-name, same-subtype Trainer text) and then a re-ingest of the local and remote D1. That is out of bounds here (the D1 is read-only for this work). It is also a data judgement: it assumes the 30th print reads exactly like its twin, which tcgdex does not confirm.

**U0252 — Rare Candy when neither deck runs the Stage 1. Deferred.** `stage2EvolvesFromBasic` (cardplay.ts) links a Stage 2 to the Basic only through a Stage 1 face in `state.cardPool`. That pool holds only the decks' ids for two reasons. First, `createGame` (setup.ts) prunes the caller's pool to the decks. Second, every pool builder (the online lobby's `loadCardPool`, the web sandbox's `fetchCardPool` and the Lua sandbox through `prepareSandbox`) loads only deck ids. So a legal 2-0-2 line (Abra + Alakazam, no Kadabra) can never use Rare Candy. §3 pins both facts. The play is refused even when the caller's pool holds the Kadabra face, and the same play succeeds once the face is in the match pool, in no zone. That shows the fix is a pool fact only. The fix needs one of two mechanisms, and the choice is a design decision across layers. The first option: `createGame` keeps the bridge Stage 1 faces, and all three builders load the faces named by each Stage 2's `evolveFrom`. The second option: a catalog-derived Stage 1 → Basic lineage table in the engine (479 entries on the local D1). That is a new runtime data file with its own generator and drift guard.

**U0646 — Hole-Digging Shovel `me03-074`.** *"Discard the top 2 cards of your deck."* With an empty deck the Item was accepted and discarded for no effect. Deck size is public, so the game state itself rules out any effect (ruling/284, the line `programPlayable` already draws for Crushing Hammer, Tool Scrapper and Night Stretcher). `trainerProgramPlayable` now refuses a Trainer whose only op is `discardDeckTop` while the milled deck is empty (the deck of the side the op names). The refusal is `NO_LEGAL_TARGET`, and the HUD greys the card through the same function (redact.ts). A deck of 1 still discards that one card. No other Trainer program is a bare deck mill. Abilities, triggers and attacks never reach this function. Mutant row: `B04-shovel-empty-deck-playable`.

Both new mutant rows were applied by hand and killed by `revisionB04.test.ts` (the reprint row also by `reprintAliasLegal.test.ts`). Registry pins: 1441 → 1448. `nonAttackRegistryIds`: 1276 → 1283. The D273 partition: 847 → 851 (the four mark-G ids are in `CENSUS_NOT_LEGAL`, by raw `legal_standard`). The search sweeps move by 3: `derivedBenchSearchMove` 145 → 148 and `revealClause` 216 → 219. Smoke run on U0252, U0257, U0258, U0548 and U0646: 36 Item runs, all ok, no unsimulated printings (4 before the fix). The B04 probe plays `30th-126` / `-127` / `-128` ok, and the Shovel into an empty deck is refused with `NO_LEGAL_TARGET`.

## B05 — Items

| U0654 | Potion `mfb-33` | bug | fixed | `B05 §1 — every Standard-legal printing of Potion runs its twin's program` |

**U0654 — Potion `mfb-33`.** This is the B04 `mfb-34` Switch defect, on its neighbour. `mfb-33` had no registry row (`programFor` reads `REGISTRY[id]` with no alias tier), so it was refused with `TRAINER_NOT_SIMULATED` ("Potion is not simulated yet") and stayed in hand. On the local D1 it is `legal_standard = 0` but `legal_standard_effective = 1`. It shares print_key `1a6334c9d86eec4e` with `sv01-188` and `me03-083`, has no regulation mark, and its text is byte-identical: *"Heal 30 damage from 1 of your Pokémon."* The fix adds `"mfb-33": POTION` next to `"mfb-34": SWITCH`. The test plays all three printings on a real board, with the Active at 50 damage. When the Active is the only Pokémon, the heal is 30 (20 left). With a Benched Pokémon too, the heal goes to the chosen one. Before the fix, the `mfb-33` rungs were red and the other two printings were green. In `reprintAliasLegal.test.ts` the Potion row moves from the D290 census to `LANDED_SINCE_EFFECTIVE_LEGALITY`. The counts become 39 identities / 73 printings (57 outside D190's sample, 82 in the union). It was the only id left in that census that is effective-legal. Registry pins: 1448 → 1449. `nonAttackRegistryIds`: 1283 → 1284. The D273 partition: 851 → 852 (`mfb-33` is in `CENSUS_NOT_LEGAL`, by raw `legal_standard`). Mutant row `B05-potion-mfb-row-dropped` was applied by hand and killed by `revisionB05.test.ts` and `reprintAliasLegal.test.ts`. Smoke run on U0654: 6 Item runs, all ok, no unsimulated printings (2 before the fix).
