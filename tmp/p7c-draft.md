# Phase 7 drafts

## 7c

- **Arrow keys move by layout (UI-PG14; decision 15).** On home, the library, builder, sandbox, online lobby and Settings the arrows move to the nearest control in their direction (`Controls.grid`, Android FocusFinder's beam rules). Tab and Shift+Tab keep list order. Down on the library's 6-column grid reaches the pile below, since a pile's Manage button is left to Tab. Where an arrow already meant something it keeps it: online's Left/Right switch the menu tabs, and Left/Right still step the sliders, option cycles and tab rows, while a field that is being edited keeps every key. The table's card ring stays linear. The library and builder draw Back first, but Tab now starts at their content and reaches Back last (`ringLast`), and Shift+Tab from nothing lands on the last control rather than the one before it. The builder gains '/' (open the card search), 'D' (focus the deck list) and '-'/'+' (one copy less or more on a focused deck row, card or row button), listed in the canvas descriptions; the shell keeps '/' from Firefox's quick find. Buttons look and read as before. 30 UI-PG14 mutant rows (one UI-PG8 row re-anchored).
- **History tooltips (UI-SM12).** After 400 ms of hover or keyboard focus on a hot-seat history icon, its spoken label (Undo turn, Undo action, New game, Redo action, Redo turn) shows in the card popup's frame (`ui/tooltip.lua`). It sits above the icon, or below or beside it when there is no room, clear of the face and inside the board in landscape, portrait and an 844x390 phone, at 12 CSS px or more. It grows in over 0.12 s with motion and appears whole under reduced motion. It is never hit-tested and goes as soon as the pointer or focus moves or an overlay opens. A disabled icon under the pointer shows it too. A touch screen's pointer (`Display.coarse`) never raises one, though keyboard focus still does. 13 UI-SM12 mutant rows.
